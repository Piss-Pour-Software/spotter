spotter

Vakyume on your iPhone writes down what you say. This hands the words to a coding agent on your Mac as you say them, and the agent's answers show up in the recording, at the line they belong to.

You need Vakyume on your iPhone first: join the free beta on TestFlight. On your Home Screen it is called Scribe for now.

Install

curl -fsSL https://spotter.pisspoursoftware.xyz/install | sh

or

brew install piss-pour-software/tap/spotter

It asks for no password and writes only under your home folder. Sign in with the email you use on your phone.

What it reads

  • Only your own recordings, with your own sign-in, kept in your Keychain.
  • No analytics, and no copy of your words on disk.
  • Words reach a model only if you give them to your agent. People near you did not choose that, so tell them it is on.

Remove it

curl -fsSL https://spotter.pisspoursoftware.xyz/install | sh -s -- --uninstall
